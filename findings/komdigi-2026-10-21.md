## Verdict

Both **exposed** (multipart parsed pre-auth, `405` from the router rather than `403` from a filter),
neither patched (no fixed release exists at time of test). `no_signal` on every diff, which is
expected pre-advisory.

## Capacity differences worth reporting

- jdih accepted a single 64 MB body and 24 MB × 3 concurrent; **no `client_max_body_size` appears to
  bite** below 64 MB.
- sikap caps bodies between **24 and 32 MB** (`413` at 32 MB) — a real edge limit jdih lacks.
- sikap leaks `server: nginx/1.18.0 (Ubuntu)`; jdih strips the version. 1.18.0 is Ubuntu 20.04's
  default → old distro base, so its PHP is likely distro-managed and, if ≤ 8.1, will get no official
  fix for this CVE.
- Neither host throttles the expensive path: only the login POST on jdih carries `throttle:3,1`.
  Static assets on jdih carry no `Cache-Control`, so every visitor revalidates against the origin.
